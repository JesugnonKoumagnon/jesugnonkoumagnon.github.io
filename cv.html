Research Papers & Working Papers

  • Working Paper · 2024 "Boko Haram Violence and Climate Change in the Lake Chad Region." District- and cell-level fixed-effects panel analysis (2009-2022, 92 districts, 4 countries) using ACLED conflict data and MODIS/CHIRPS climate data; finds drought and temperature anomalies predict conflict intensity, with the effect nearly doubling in high-cropland districts. Presented at ASE, Abomey-Calavi, 2024.
  • Policy Brief · 2025 "Multidimensional Poverty and Social Protection Assistance in Nigeria." State-level analysis (37 states) merging NBS poverty data with social-register and program-beneficiary data; finds 47.4% of Nigeria's poor are unregistered for assistance, with the highest-poverty states receiving the lowest program coverage. Available on request.
  • High-Frequency Checks for RCT Field Monitoring, Benin Savings RCT. Stata pipeline for real-time data-quality monitoring during a savings-behavior RCT: duplicate detection, skip-pattern validation, and an attrition funnel, built to catch enumeration issues during fieldwork.
